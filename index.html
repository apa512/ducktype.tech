No. 02 · Developer tool02/02

Cowboy for Herdr

Does any agent need me? Answered from your phone.

For developers who run AI coding agents in Herdr. Cowboy connects to your own computer over SSH and shows every agent's status and latest output, with the ones waiting for an answer at the top. It stays read-only until you switch input on, and it has no account, no cloud relay and no analytics.

§ 02 · The company

Who we are

A small software company with a narrow focus: a few apps, built with care and looked after for the long run.

Duck Type LLC makes apps for people to use every day, and distributes them through the App Store and Google Play.

The company was founded by Erik Strömberg, a full-stack developer who spent years building web, data and blockchain systems for other companies. Duck Type is where that experience goes into products of our own.

Design, development, publishing and support all happen in-house, so a support email reaches the people who built the app.

  1. No ads, no tracking.Neither of our apps shows advertising or contains analytics or tracking SDKs.
  2. Your data stays yours.We keep only what a feature needs, never sell it, and let you delete it from inside the app.
  3. Supported for real.Every app has a support page, a privacy policy and an email address that a person answers.
