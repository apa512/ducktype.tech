Contact us

Email support@ducktype.tech. To help us help you quickly, include:

  • which app, and its version (shown in the App Store or Google Play listing, or in the app's settings);
  • your device model and its iOS or Android version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

For business, partnership or press enquiries, write to erik@ducktype.tech.

Purchases and refunds

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, so they handle payments and refunds:

  • App Store: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Google Play: follow Google's refund instructions.
  • New phone or reinstalled the app? Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with and use Restore purchases in the app.

If a purchase went through but the app does not recognise it, email us with the app name and the date of purchase.

Deleting your data

  • Yatzy Pop! online account: in the app, go to Online and choose delete account. It takes effect immediately. The account deletion page explains what is removed.
  • Everything stored on your device by either app is removed when you uninstall it.
  • Cowboy for Herdr sends us nothing, so there is nothing on our side to delete.

For any other data request, see our privacy policy or email us.
